Privacy policy

Your files stay yours.

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Grabi is an open-source Android media toolkit maintained by Raisoft Studio. This policy explains what information the Grabi website and Android application handle.

Information we do not collect

Grabi does not require an account and does not intentionally collect your name, email address, download history, media library, browser history, VPN configurations or playback history. The Android app is designed to keep this information on your device.

Information processed on your device

The app processes URLs, downloaded files, playback data, browser sessions and VPN configuration files only to provide the features you request. Files and app settings are stored locally unless you choose to share or upload them through another service.

Website services

This website may be hosted by Vercel, which can process standard server logs such as IP address, browser type and request time. The download buttons query the public GitHub Releases API to find the newest APK. Google Fonts may receive a request when the font stylesheet is loaded. These services have their own privacy policies.

Permissions and third-party providers

Android permissions are requested only for features such as storage, notifications, network access, media playback, browser downloads and VPN operation. When you visit a provider site, use a VPN endpoint or download media, that provider may process information under its own terms. Grabi does not control those services.

Children and changes

Grabi is not directed at children under 13. We may update this policy when the website or app changes. The date above identifies the current version.

Questions

For questions about this policy or the project, open a discussion or issue on GitHub.
