Private by design

Your files stay
yours.

Grabi gives you useful tools without turning your media into a product. No account wall, no ad profile and no mystery about where your files go. Your library and your playback choices remain under your control.

Read the privacy policy ↗
⌂
On-device first

Downloads, playback history and browser sessions are designed to stay on your Android device. Grabi stores the things you need for the app to work, not a remote copy of your life.

◌
No account wall

Use the core app without creating an account or handing over a profile to us. Start grabbing, playing and browsing without another sign-up flow.

✓
Open and inspectable

Review the source, permissions and release notes on GitHub before installing. Grabi is built to be questioned, improved and understood.
